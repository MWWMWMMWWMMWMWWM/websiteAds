Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how NotARealGameDev ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our game A Journey Rediscovered - Intro Version ("the Game") or interact with our website and services.

🔑 Key Point: We do NOT collect, store, or process any personal data from our users. This policy outlines the minimal data interactions that occur through third-party platforms.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you use the Game, you are not required to provide any personal information directly to us. We do not operate user accounts, registration systems, or data collection mechanisms within the Game.

1.2 Information Collected by Epic Games

The Game is distributed through the Epic Games Store. When you play the Game, your Epic Games account information may be accessed through Epic Online Services. This includes:

  • Account ID – A unique identifier for your Epic Games account
  • Display Name – Your publicly visible username
  • Language Preference – Your selected language settings
  • Linked Account Display Names – Names of accounts linked to your Epic account (e.g., Steam, PlayStation, Xbox)
  • Online Status – Your presence status (online/offline/away)
  • Friends List – Your list of friends on the Epic Games platform
  • Blocked Players – Players you have blocked

Important: This information is collected and managed by Epic Games, not by us. We do not store, process, or have access to this data beyond what is necessary for the Game's functionality (such as displaying your friends list or online status). Epic Games' handling of your data is governed by their own Privacy Policy.

1.3 Information Collected via the Website

Our website (notarealgamedev.com) hosts:

  • A feedback form – You may voluntarily submit feedback or survey responses. These submissions are sent to a secure server and may be reviewed by us for the purpose of improving the Game. Submissions are anonymous and do not collect any personally identifiable information.

1.4 No Analytics or Tracking

We do not use analytics tools, cookies, tracking pixels, or any other method to track your behavior across the web or within the Game.

2. How We Use Information

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not use your information for any purpose. The only data we receive is:

  • Voluntary survey responses – Used solely to improve the Game experience. These responses are anonymous.
  • Game functionality data – Epic Online Services data is used only to enable features like friend lists and online status within the Game. We do not store or retain this data.

3. Data Retention

We do not retain any personal data. Survey responses are stored in a secure database but contain no personally identifiable information. We may retain aggregate, anonymized feedback for quality improvement purposes.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties. The only third-party involved is Epic Games, which handles data as described in Section 1.2.

5. Data Security

While we do not collect personal data, we take reasonable measures to protect any information you voluntarily submit (e.g., survey responses) against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Game is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • Access – The right to request access to your data
  • Correction – The right to request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion – The right to request deletion of your data
  • Restriction – The right to restrict processing of your data
  • Data Portability – The right to receive your data in a structured format

Since we do not collect personal data, these rights generally do not apply to our services. However, if you believe we hold any of your data, please contact us using the details below.

For data controlled by Epic Games, please refer to Epic Games' Privacy Policy for information on how to exercise your rights regarding your Epic Games account data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting on this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. The date at the top of this policy indicates the last revision.

10. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Norwegian Personal Data Act (Personopplysningsloven), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access – You may request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification – You may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure – You may request deletion of your data.
  • Right to restrict processing – You may request restriction of processing.
  • Right to data portability – You may request transfer of your data.
  • Right to object – You may object to processing of your data.

Since Licensor does not collect personal data, these rights generally do not apply to our services. However, if you believe we hold any personal data, please contact us at AJRBugs@proton.me.

Why it matters

  • EU: the European Accessibility Act has applied since 28 June 2025, including to online shops. Service businesses with fewer than 10 staff and at most €2M annual turnover or balance-sheet total are exempt.
  • Norway: the universal-design rules for ICT also cover private businesses' websites aimed at the public. The regulator can order fixes and impose coercive fines if errors aren't fixed.
  • People: those with low vision, older users, people using a screen reader or keyboard, and anyone reading a phone in sunlight.

The six error types we fix

  1. Text with too little contrast
  2. Images without a text alternative (alt text)
  3. Form fields without a label
  4. Links without text
  5. Buttons without text
  6. Pages without a declared language

Source: WebAIM Million 2026, a yearly check of one million home pages.

How it works

  1. Free check of up to five pages. You get a report of what's wrong and where.
  2. Fix pack: exact changes for each page template, with passing colors, alt texts, labels, and link and button text. We apply them with temporary access, or hand them to your developer.
  3. Check afterwards: a new automated check, a manual check of your key pages (keyboard and screen reader), and a before-and-after report.

What we don't promise: that your site becomes "100% compliant". Automated checks find only part of the problems, so we tell you plainly what remains. We don't sell overlay or widget products either. We fix the actual code.

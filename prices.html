Terms

  • Fixed prices, the same for everyone. All prices exclude VAT (MVA); VAT is added where the law requires it.
  • Where a NOK price is shown, it applies when you pay in NOK. Prices shown only in USD can be invoiced in NOK at the day's exchange rate on the order date.
  • “From” prices: the final price depends on the size of the job, such as the number of pages, sources or the data volume, and is agreed in writing before work starts.
  • Monthly services run month to month with no minimum term. Cancel by e-mail at any time; it takes effect at the end of the paid month.
  • Clients registered in low- and middle-income countries get a published discount: 60% (low income), 40% (lower-middle) and 20% (upper-middle income), by the World Bank's classification.
  • One free item per business: the accessibility check or the first tender shortlist.
  • Every delivery includes free fixes when the result doesn't match what was agreed. If we don't deliver what we agreed, we fix it free or refund you.
  • These offers are for businesses only. We don't work for anyone under Norwegian, EU or UN sanctions.

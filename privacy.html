Who is responsible

What we collect, and why

  • When you contact us or become a client: your name, e-mail address, organization and what you write to us. We use it to answer you and to deliver what we agreed (contract or legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).
  • When we contact businesses: we use only general addresses (such as info@ or contact@) that the business has published itself, and we write only when we have a specific reason to think the offer is relevant. The basis is legitimate interest. You can ask us to stop at any time. We will, and we'll keep the address only on a do-not-contact list so that we never write again.
  • When we look for needs on the web: we read public posts where people ask for help or look for tools, through search engines and through public feeds and APIs where a site allows it. We follow robots.txt and each site's terms. We note the need in our own words, without names, usernames or contact details. A link to the post is kept for at most 30 days, and only while it's in use. We never store health or other special-category information. The basis is legitimate interest: finding out what people need so we can build it.

AI and other providers

We use Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, to write and analyze text, and DeepL or Google Translate for translation. E-mail runs through Zoho Mail (Zoho's EU data centre; the mail is stored in the EU). Where a provider processes data outside the EEA, the transfer relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses; GitHub and Google also take part in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. We never sell personal data.

Visits to this website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which logs visitors' IP addresses for security. GitHub doesn't state how long it keeps these logs. The basis is legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): keeping the website secure and running. We use no analytics tools or tracking cookies. The feedback form on the home page and the game A Journey Rediscovered are covered by the game's privacy policy.

How long we keep data

  • Client data: for as long as the agreement lasts, and accounting records for as long as Norwegian bookkeeping law requires (legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Inquiries that don't lead to an agreement: deleted after 6 months.
  • General addresses we wrote to without getting a reply: deleted after 3 months.
  • The do-not-contact list (addresses that said no): for as long as we send any messages, so a "no" is always respected.
  • Links from looking for needs on the web: at most 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and data portability, and you can object to the processing. We handle objections at once, and we always honor an objection to direct marketing. Our legitimate-interest assessment is available on request. Write to post@notarealgamedev.com. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.
